I'm Rebecca, a PhD candidate in Earth Sciences
at the University at Buffalo.


With my advisor Elizabeth Thomas, I use computer models and field observations to investigate how lake sediments preserve climate information. Paleoclimate data like these allow us to learn how climate changed in the past so we can better predict future climate changes.
